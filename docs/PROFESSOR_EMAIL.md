**Subject:** In-silico complexity-science pipeline (NSTG-constrained) — not a clinical claim

Dear Professor —,

I wanted to share a small, installable research codebase we stood up for an
in-silico complexity-science study, not a care tool.

It does three things, all computational:

1. **NSTG-guided index** — structured *placeholder* summaries for
   oncology-adjacent supportive themes and Nigerian comorbidity contexts
   (malaria, HIV, sickle cell disease, anaemia), with an explicit provenance
   note pointing at the official FMoH *Nigeria Standard Treatment Guidelines*
   (3rd ed., 2022). We do not redistribute NSTG text.
2. **Pathology dynamics** — a documented four-state nonlinear ODE (burden,
   immune competence, toxicity, biologic exposure) with three computational
   archetypes (basins), not diagnoses.
3. **Constrained pathway search** — class-level biologic effectors
   (checkpoint-like, TGF-β-trap-like, cytokine-like, fusion-mAb-like) ranked
   under toxicity caps and comorbidity-shaped constraints, plus a
   falsification checklist.

Outputs are JSON/Markdown *in-silico hypotheses*. The software is not a
medical device, not CDS, not dosing advice, and is awaiting external
validation. There is a versioned JSON contract if we later call this from
Project Confluence; the two codebases stay separate.

If useful, I can walk through the ODE, the constraint map, and what would
actually constitute a validation plan.

With thanks,
