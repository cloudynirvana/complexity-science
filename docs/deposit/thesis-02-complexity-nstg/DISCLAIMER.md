# Disclaimer

**Thesis #2 — Complexity Science and NSTG-Guided In-Silico Pathology Dynamics**
is an in-silico / computational research manuscript. It is **not** a medical
device, **not** clinical decision support (CDS), **not** a dosing advisor,
and **not** a cure.

## Epistemic boundary (non-negotiable)

```text
Knowledge ≠ Evidence ≠ Mechanism ≠ Parameter ≠ Prediction
```

Nigeria Standard Treatment Guidelines (NSTG, 3rd edition, 2022) appear here
only as a **structured clinical-knowledge constraint layer** for pathway
exploration. NSTG is **not** a source of ODE coefficients. Official NSTG
text, the Nigeria Essential Medicines List, the National Cancer Control Plan
2018–2022, and the National ChemoSafe policy are **cited, not redistributed**.
NSTG statements are **never** auto-translated into model parameters, doses,
rate constants, ODE coefficients, or predictions.

If a line in this repository conflicts with official NSTG 2022, **NSTG wins**.

## What this thesis does

It specifies and reports a data-first in-silico architecture (CaseCard,
parameter-smuggling refusal, closed evidence gate, PathwaySketch ranking of
bibliographic hypotheses). Results are architectural. No patient data were
used. Named public datasets are future binds, not silent analyses.

## What this thesis does not do

- Diagnose, treat, prevent, cure, or manage any person.
- Recommend a biologic product, dose, schedule, or combination.
- Claim clinical validation, Phase II status, or regulator readiness.
- Execute NSTG as a care protocol.
- Fit or smuggle ODE / PK / PD parameters from a CaseCard.
- Speak for the Federal Ministry of Health, Nigeria, or for any manufacturer.

Use is at your own risk. If you are a patient or caregiver, consult a
licensed clinician and the official NSTG. If you are a researcher, treat
every sketch as a hypothesis awaiting independent falsification.
