Abstract

Complex pathological systems sit in Weaver's class of organised complexity: metabolic competition, immune exclusion, hypoxic and invasive niches, stromal delivery barriers, and latent residual disease. Biologics pathway exploration is therefore a systemic control problem, not a product-selection problem. This computational research thesis — not a clinical study — specifies a data-only in-silico pipeline in which a YAML CaseCard holds knowledge, evidence pointers, candidate mechanisms, falsifiers, and qualitative Nigeria Standard Treatment Guidelines (NSTG) constraints. The non-negotiable boundary is Knowledge ≠ Evidence ≠ Mechanism ≠ Parameter ≠ Prediction. NSTG 2022 is a structured clinical-knowledge constraint layer. It is never auto-translated into ODE coefficients, doses, or predictions, and official guideline text is cited rather than redistributed. A stub PathwaySketch explorer ranks bibliographic, falsifiable hypotheses and always refuses parameterisation. Four seed cases (TNBC, GBM, PDAC, dormant/occult disease) validate as research objects. Parameter smuggling, including numeric NSTG scales, is refused. Named public datasets are listed only as future binds. Headed sections state the Problem Statement, Justification of the Study, and Significance of the Study. NSTG is a knowledge constraint, not a source of ODE coefficients. Not a medical device.
