# Disclaimer

**Complexity Science** is an in-silico / computational research scaffold.
It is **not** a medical device, **not** clinical decision support (CDS),
**not** a dosing advisor, and **not** a cure.

## Epistemic boundary (non-negotiable)

```text
Knowledge ≠ Evidence ≠ Mechanism ≠ Parameter ≠ Prediction
```

Nigeria Standard Treatment Guidelines (NSTG) appear here only as a
**structured clinical-knowledge constraint layer** for pathway exploration.
NSTG text is **not redistributed**. NSTG statements are **never**
auto-translated into model parameters, doses, rate constants, or predictions.

## What this software does

It validates `CaseCard` YAML and emits a gated `PathwaySketch`: ranked
research hypotheses, required evidence, and an explicit list of refused
non-parameters. No patient data enter the pipeline.

## What this software does not do

- Diagnose, treat, prevent, cure, or manage any person.
- Recommend a biologic product, dose, schedule, or combination.
- Claim clinical validation, Phase II status, or regulator readiness.
- Execute NSTG as a care protocol.
- Fit or smuggle ODE / PK / PD parameters from a CaseCard.

Use is at your own risk. If you are a patient or caregiver, consult a
licensed clinician and the official NSTG. If you are a researcher, treat
every sketch as a hypothesis awaiting independent falsification.
