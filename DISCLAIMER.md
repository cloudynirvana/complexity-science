# Disclaimer

**Complexity Science** is in-silico research software. It is **not** a medical
device, **not** clinical decision support, **not** a treatment or dosing
advisor, and **not** a substitute for Nigeria Standard Treatment Guidelines
(NSTG), clinician judgment, or any regulator.

## What this software does

It integrates a compact, documented nonlinear dynamical system and ranks
**computational** pathway hypotheses under toxicity and comorbidity-shaped
constraints. Those hypotheses are starting points for further modelling and
wet-lab or clinical-protocol design by qualified investigators — they are not
care recommendations.

## What this software does not do

- Diagnose, treat, cure, prevent, or manage any disease.
- Recommend a biologic product, dose, schedule, or combination for a person.
- Claim external, prospective, or regulatory validation.
- Redistribute the official NSTG text (see `docs/NSTG_PROVENANCE.md`).
- Speak for the Federal Ministry of Health, Nigeria, or any manufacturer.

## Language we do not use

Reports and docs must not claim that outputs are a **cure**, **Phase II**,
**FDA-ready**, or **clinically validated**. See `complexity_science/honesty.py`.

Use is at your own risk. No warranty of fitness for any purpose is offered.
If you are a patient or caregiver, consult a licensed clinician and the
official NSTG. If you are a researcher, treat every numeric output as a
model artefact until independently falsified.
